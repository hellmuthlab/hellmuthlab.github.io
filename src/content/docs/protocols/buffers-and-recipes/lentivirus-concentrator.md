## Purpose

Concentration of lentiviral supernatant without ultracentrifugation, replacing commercial PEG-based
reagents such as PEG-it. Used in [Lentivirus preparation](/protocols/cell-culture/lentivirus-preparation/).

The recipe and the matching concentration protocol are those of the **Functional Genomics Core at the
University of Texas MD Anderson Cancer Center**, published on their
[core facility resources page](https://www.mdanderson.org/research/research-resources/core-facilities/functional-genomics-core/resources.html).

## Materials

| Component | Stock concentration | Amount for 200 ml | Supplier (cat. no.) |
|---|---|---|---|
| PEG-8000 | 40 % (w/v) | 80 g | to be added |
| NaCl | 1.2 M | 14 g | to be added |
| 10× PBS, pH 7.4 | 1× | 20 ml | to be added |
| Milli-Q H<sub>2</sub>O | | 80 ml, then to 200 ml | — |

## Preparation

1. Dissolve 80 g PEG-8000 and 14 g NaCl in 80 ml Milli-Q H<sub>2</sub>O and 20 ml 10× PBS (pH 7.4).
2. Stir gently until the solids have dissolved; warm slightly if needed.
3. Adjust the pH to 7.0–7.2.
4. Bring the final volume to 200 ml with Milli-Q H<sub>2</sub>O.
5. Sterilize by filtration through a 0.2 µm filter.

## Storage

4 °C.

## Use

Add **1 volume of concentrator to 3 volumes of cleared viral supernatant**, giving final concentrations of
10 % (w/v) PEG-8000 and 0.3 M NaCl. Incubate at 4 °C for at least 4 h (better overnight), then pellet at
1600 × g for 60 min at 4 °C. See [Lentivirus preparation](/protocols/cell-culture/lentivirus-preparation/)
for the full procedure.
