## Day 1 — Plating

The protocol below mainly follows the RNAi Consortium (TRC) / Broad Institute protocol for lentivirus production. That protocol is very detailed and, judging by its version notes, has been continuously optimized. It is available at the [Broad GPP portal](https://portals.broadinstitute.org/gpp/public/resources/protocols) (*TRC shRNA sgRNA ORF Low Throughput Viral Production, 2015-06*).

**General remarks**

- Lentivirus collected at day 2 after infection is more potent than lentivirus collected at day 3.
- To speed up the procedure, HEK293T cells can also be transfected in the morning. The first media change (to high FBS) is performed in the evening of the same day, and the lentivirus supernatant is collected the following day.
- For large-scale preps (10 cm dish and above), transfection reagents like Lipofectamine, FuGENE or TransIT become too expensive. Unless efficiency has to be maximized (e.g. for large transfer vectors like Cas9 constructs), use PEI or calcium-phosphate transfection instead.

**Plating**

- 48-well: plate 0.6×10<sup>5</sup> HEK293T in 0.3 ml DMEM (cell suspension at 2×10<sup>5</sup>/ml)
- 6-well: plate 5×10<sup>5</sup> HEK293T in 2.5 ml DMEM (cell suspension at 2×10<sup>5</sup>/ml)
- 10 cm dish: plate 3×10<sup>6</sup> HEK293T in 10 ml (cell suspension at 3×10<sup>5</sup>/ml)

| | 96-well (0.35 cm²) | 48-well (0.9 cm²) | 24-well (1.9 cm²) | 12-well (3.8 cm²) | 6-well (9.6 cm²) | 10 cm dish (59 cm²) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Volume | 0.1 ml | 0.3 ml | 0.6 ml | 1.2 ml | 2.5 ml | 10 ml |
| Cell number | 0.2×10<sup>5</sup> | 0.6×10<sup>5</sup> | 1.2×10<sup>5</sup> | 2.4×10<sup>5</sup> | 5×10<sup>5</sup> | 3×10<sup>6</sup> |
| Cell suspension | 2×10<sup>5</sup>/ml | 2×10<sup>5</sup>/ml | 2×10<sup>5</sup>/ml | 2×10<sup>5</sup>/ml | 2×10<sup>5</sup>/ml | 3×10<sup>5</sup>/ml |

## Day 2 — Transfection

The transfection protocol combines elements of the TransIT-LT1 manufacturer's protocol and the original Lipofectamine 2000 protocol, with these key points and changes:

- A 1:3:4 ratio of pMD2.G : psPAX2 : transfer vector is used, as suggested by an in-house protocol and the Addgene protocol for pLKO.1 (#10878).
- The amounts of DNA and transfection reagent for 10 cm dishes are reduced compared to the manufacturer's protocol (i.e. not scaled up proportionally from 6-well). This reduction was suggested by the in-house protocol and may be feasible due to the reduced culture volume (10 ml vs 15 ml).
- The total DNA (~100 ng) for the 96-well protocol with TransIT is taken from the TransIT manufacturer's protocol (1:3 ratio).

| | 96-well (0.35 cm²) | 48-well (0.9 cm²) | 24-well (1.9 cm²) | 12-well (3.8 cm²) | 6-well (9.6 cm²) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| pMD2.G (EX01) | 12 ng | 36 ng | 70 ng | 140 ng | 300 ng |
| psPAX2 (EX02) | 36 ng | 108 ng | 210 ng | 420 ng | 900 ng |
| transfer plasmid | 48 ng | 144 ng | 280 ng | 560 ng | 1200 ng |
| **total DNA** | 96 ng | 288 ng | 560 ng | 1120 ng | 2.4 µg |
| TransIT-LT1 | 0.3 µl | 0.9 µl | 1.8 µl | 3.6 µl | 7.2 µl |
| Optimem (total) | 9 µl | 27 µl | 54 µl | 108 µl | 250 µl |

**10 cm dish protocols**

- For large transfer vectors, intensifying the transfection and/or changing the ratios can help (see below).
- High intensity (45 µl TransIT) corresponds to the TransIT manufacturer's protocol.
- Use a 1:3:6 ratio for large transfer vectors (e.g. Cas9 constructs).

| | low intensity I (1:3:4) | low intensity II (1:3:6) | moderate intensity (1:3:4) | high intensity (1:3:4) | high intensity (1:3:6) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| pMD2.G (EX01) | 1 µg | 1 µg | 1.5 µg | 1.9 µg | 1.5 µg |
| psPAX2 (EX02) | 3 µg | 3 µg | 4.5 µg | 5.7 µg | 4.5 µg |
| transfer plasmid | 4 µg | 6 µg | 6 µg | 7.6 µg | 9 µg |
| **total DNA** | 8 µg | 10 µg | 12 µg | ~15 µg | 15 µg |
| TransIT-LT1 | 24 µl | 30 µl | 36 µl | 45 µl | 45 µl |
| Optimem (total) | 1.5 ml | 1.5 ml | 1.5 ml | 1.5 ml | 1.5 ml |

**Procedure** (generally follows the original Lipofectamine 2000 protocol)

- [ ] **1.** Dilute DNA in Optimem (half of the final volume, e.g. 125 µl for 6-well).
- [ ] **2.** Dilute TransIT in Optimem (half of the final volume, e.g. 125 µl for 6-well) and incubate for 5 min (max. 30 min).
- [ ] **3.** Combine both dilutions, mix gently and incubate at room temperature for 15–30 min.
- [ ] **4.** Apply the TransIT:DNA complexes to the cells drop-wise, to different areas of the well, and distribute by rocking the plate gently.

**Mastermixes** for steps 1 and 2 are usually set up as follows (6-well example):

| DNA mastermix | 1-fold | 6-fold |
|---|---|---|
| psPAX2 | 900 ng | 5400 ng |
| pMD2.G | 300 ng | 1800 ng |
| transfer plasmid (pLKO) | 1200 ng | added to each virus individually |
| Optimem | 125 µl | 750 µl |

The DNA:Optimem mastermix for packaging and envelope plasmids is distributed to individual tubes (6-well: 125 µl per virus), and the indicated amount of transfer vector is added.

| TransIT mastermix | 1-fold | 6-fold |
|---|---|---|
| TransIT-LT1 | 7.2 µl | 43.2 µl |
| Optimem | 125 µl | 750 µl |

- [ ] After ~5 min incubation (max. 30 min) of the TransIT:Optimem mastermix, add the indicated amount (6-well: 125 µl) to each DNA:Optimem mix and incubate at room temperature for 15–30 min.
- [ ] Add the TransIT:DNA:Optimem mix to the cells, distributing it as evenly as possible.

## Day 3 — Medium change

- [ ] 12 h after transfection, change the medium to high-BSA or high-FBS (20 %) virus-production medium (volumes below).

| | 96-well | 48-well | 24-well | 12-well | 6-well | 10 cm dish |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Volume per supernatant change | 125 µl | 300 µl | 750 µl | 1.5 ml | 3 ml | 15 ml |
| Final / total supernatant volume | 250 µl | 600 µl | 1.5 ml | 3 ml | 6 ml | 30 ml |

## Day 4 — Collect supernatant 1

- [ ] Transfer the supernatant (SN) to a collection tube (15 ml or 50 ml) and store at 4 °C.
- [ ] Add fresh virus-production medium (**3 ml per 6-well, 15 ml per 10 cm dish**).

## Day 5 — Collect supernatant 2

- [ ] Transfer the SN to the collection tube, pooling it with the day 4 SN.
- [ ] Remove HEK293T cells, either by
  - centrifugation at 3000 g for 15 min (4 °C) (taken from the Retro-Concentin protocol), or
  - filtration through a 0.45 µm filter (location of the filters: to be added).

At this point the lentivirus supernatant can be used for a “fresh infection” or concentrated with PEG-it.

**PEG-it concentration**

- [ ] Add 5× PEG-it to the SN.
- [ ] Mix by inverting and store at 4 °C overnight (12 h to a few days).

## Day 6 — Concentrate

- [ ] Centrifuge the PEG-it:SN mix at 1500 g and 4 °C for 30 min.
- [ ] Resuspend the pellet in PBS and store at −80 °C.
- Standard stock concentration is 20× (e.g. 300 µl PBS for a 6-well when 6 ml SN have been collected).

## Comparison of published protocols

Available protocols differ widely in the ratios of packaging/envelope to transfer plasmids:

| | Broad (10 cm) | Broad (6-well) | In-house (10 cm) | Trono lab (10 cm) | Addgene (6 cm) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| pMD2.G | 0.9 µg | 50 ng | 1 µg | 39.5 µg | 250 ng |
| psPAX2 | 9 µg | 500 ng | 3 µg | 73 µg | 750 ng |
| pLKO | 9 µg | 500 ng | 4 µg | 112.5 µg | 1000 ng |
| ratio | 1 : 10 : 10 | 1 : 10 : 10 | 1 : 3 : 4 | 1 : 1.8 : 2.8 | 1 : 3 : 4 |
| Transfection reagent | 54 µl (TransIT) | 3 µl (TransIT) | 24 µl (FuGENE) | CaCl<sub>2</sub> | 6 µl (FuGENE) |
| DNA : reagent | 1 µg : 2.7 µl | 1 µg : 3 µl | 1 µg : 3 µl | n/a | 1 µg : 3 µl |

The ratio of a 10 cm dish (59 cm²) to a 6-well (9.6 cm²) is ~6:1. The Broad protocols, with a ~20:1 ratio for DNA and a ~18:1 ratio for the transfection reagent, are therefore highly inconsistent.

The TransIT-LT1 routine protocol recommends a DNA : reagent ratio of 1:3:

| | 6-well | 10 cm |
|---|---|---|
| DNA | 2.5 µg | 15 µg |
| TransIT | 7.5 µl | 45 µl |
