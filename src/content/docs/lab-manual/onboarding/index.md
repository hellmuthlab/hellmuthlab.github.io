Welcome to the Hellmuth Lab!  
Getting started requires some **administrative steps** and getting you into our **IT setup**. If you don't have one already, getting your **LRZ username** as soon as possible is key: without it you cannot log into our chat (LMUchat), the lab notebook or the cloud storage. So please take care of the visiting agreement first.

## Admin

Whether you are employed via the DKFZ or are a student at LMU, you need a **Hospitationsvertrag** (visiting/observership agreement) with the LMU University Hospital to work in the ELLF. Only with this agreement do you receive IT access, namely your MedVer username and, more importantly, your **LRZ username**.

Once you have joined, our ELLF administrator, Doris, will give you the checklists and forms required to start working in the ELLF. These include:

- Doctoral student and staff registration form (*Doktoranden- und Mitarbeitermeldeformular*)
- Workplace safety briefing for all staff (*Einarbeitung Arbeitssicherheit für alle Mitarbeiter*)
- Orientation checklist for new staff (*Einarbeitung neuer Mitarbeiter*)
- Equipment instruction (*Geräteunterweisung*)

## IT setup

To get you up and running, you need to be logged into our apps and resources. Tell Johannes your **LRZ username** as soon as you have it; most of the steps below depend on it.

### LMUchat (Matrix / Element)

We use [LMUchat](https://www.lmu.de/de/die-lmu/struktur/zentrale-universitaetsverwaltung/it-services-division/it-servicedesk/zentrale-it-angebote/lmu-chat-matrix/) (Matrix/Element) for all communication and collaboration in the lab. It works on any PC, laptop or smartphone, in the browser as well as in the app. You will receive an invitation to the Hellmuth Lab space from Johannes.

### eLabFTW

[eLabFTW](https://elab.ub.lmu.de/login.php) is our electronic lab notebook. Send Johannes your LRZ username (required) and he will invite you to our lab team.

### Benchling

We use [Benchling](https://benchling.com/signin/welcome) only for its molecular biology tools (plasmid maps, virtual cloning, Sanger sequencing alignments, etc.), **not** as a lab notebook. Join our organization `hellmuth-lab` to access our shared resources.

### Zotero

[Zotero](https://www.zotero.org/) is a free, open-source reference manager. We also use it to share and read papers. Join our [Zotero group](https://www.zotero.org/groups/6035832/hellmuth_lab).

### LRZ Cloud Storage

[LRZ Cloud Storage](https://doku.lrz.de/cloud-storage-10745690.html) is our primary storage for data, resources and protocols. Access requires an LRZ username, and the lab admin (Johannes) must grant it.

- **On your computer:** within the Munich Scientific Network (campus Wi-Fi / Eduroam or VPN) you can mount the storage as a network drive. Johannes will send you the exact path.
- **In the browser:** use [WebDisk](https://webdisk.ads.mwn.de) with your LRZ login.
- **Documentation:** see the [LRZ Cloud Storage documentation](https://doku.lrz.de/cloud-storage-10745690.html) for setup instructions on all operating systems.

Every team member has a personal home folder inside the lab space. Please store all files according to the recommended folder structure, for example original data in `01_Data`, analyses in `02_Analysis` and results in `03_Results`. Use descriptive file names to ensure reproducibility, traceability and efficient collaboration.
